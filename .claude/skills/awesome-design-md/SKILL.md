# awesome-design-md

Source: https://github.com/VoltAgent/awesome-design-md (see LICENSE).

Each folder in `design-md/<brand>/DESIGN.md` documents one site's colours, type, spacing, components and layout.
Read the one or two closest to the brief for calibration. Do not reproduce another brand's identity, name, or assets.
