# Narration voice

Generated locally with VoiceStudio (https://github.com/debpalash/VoiceStudio), engine OmniVoice (k2-fsa/OmniVoice).

- Voice design: `female, middle-aged, low pitch, indian accent` (seed 11). One reference clip was designed, then every line was voice-cloned from it so the timbre stays constant.
- Hindi lines (5, 10, 36, 37) were written in Devanagari and generated with `language=hi`.
- Speed 0.93, 32 steps, CPU.
