Antibiotic plans for sepsis

Empiric cover by source and MDR risk, then a day-3 plan from the cultures. Follows the Surviving Sepsis Campaign and IDSA guidance.

Live in the app

  1. Hour 0

    Severe community-acquired pneumonia: a beta-lactam such as ceftriaxone plus a macrolide. Add MRSA or Pseudomonas cover only when risk factors are present.

  2. Day 3 review

    S. pneumoniae sensitive to ceftriaxone: stop the broader agents and plan a 5 to 7 day course if the patient is improving.
